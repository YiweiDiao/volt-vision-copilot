# Safety Limitations

Power-signature screening cannot confirm tool wear, machine failure, root cause, diagnosis, or maintenance necessity.

Volt Vision Copilot does not provide automatic machine control, shutdown, PLC/SCADA/MES interaction, tuning, repair instructions, or ticket creation.

Only authorized maintenance or production representatives may decide on
real-world actions under local procedures.

Use safe language throughout review:

- suspected deviation
- manual review
- evidence recording
- recurrence checking
- escalation
